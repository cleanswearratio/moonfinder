Moon sign finder

Privacy

Short version: your birth date, time, and city are used only in your browser to work out your sign. Nothing about them is sent anywhere unless you ask for your full profile — and even then, we send less than you might expect.

What stays in your browser

The birth date, time, and city you enter on the form are used only to calculate a result on your own device. That calculation — a lookup against precomputed astronomical tables — never leaves your browser, and reaching the reveal screen involves no network request at all. We do not see your birth date, your birth time, or the city you entered, whether or not you go on to request the full profile.

What we collect, and what we send

If you request the full profile, we ask for your email address. When you submit it, your browser sends us:

  • the email address you typed
  • your moon sign, and a second candidate sign if your birth time leaves it ambiguous
  • your sun sign
  • the year you were born (not the month or day)
  • the time zone your birth city uses (for example America/New_York), not the city name itself
  • whether you told us your exact birth time or not

That is the complete list. Your birth date, birth time, and city name are not part of it — only the sign result and the birth year are. We store no birth data anywhere except what is listed above.

Where it goes

Everything above goes to our email service provider, which stores your contact details and sends you the profile you asked for. We do not run our own database — that provider is the only place your email address is kept. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except that provider, who sends the email on our behalf.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The only network requests it makes are for the page itself, the tables it uses to calculate signs, and — only if you submit the email form — the one request that sends your details to our email provider.

How to stop hearing from us

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, which removes you immediately. You can also reply to any email we send you and ask us to remove your details by hand.

One caveat

This page describes what the code actually does, honestly, but it is not legal advice. If you are sending this to visitors in the EU or another jurisdiction with its own rules, have someone qualified check this page before you rely on it.
